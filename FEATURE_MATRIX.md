# Feature Matrix: RecruitOS Workflow

## Core Pipeline
Everything must revolve around this strict, linear flow:
1. **Lead** (Initial capture from marketing or Field BDA)
2. **Interview Scheduled** (Office BDA sets the date)
3. **Selected** (Candidate passes the interview)
4. **Recharge** (Candidate commits/pays/onboards)
5. **Joined** (Final success state)

## Role-Based Feature Matrix

### 1. Field BDA (Mobile-First Focus)
**Goal:** Speed and simplicity on the field.
- **Lead Capture Form:** Hyper-optimized, large tap targets, minimal fields (Name, Phone, Location).
- **Today's Locations:** View assigned areas.
- **My Captured Leads:** Simple list of leads added today.
- **NO** marketing data, **NO** complex scheduling, **NO** analytics.

### 2. Office BDA (Desktop-First Focus)
**Goal:** High-volume processing and follow-ups.
- **Kanban Board:** Drag and drop candidates through the pipeline.
- **Call Queue:** Auto-loading list of leads to call today.
- **Scheduling:** One-click interview scheduling.
- **Follow-up Reminders:** Clear daily tasks.
- **Candidate Timeline:** View all history and notes.

### 3. Marketing (Independent Module)
**Goal:** Track ROI and Lead flow.
- **Campaign Dashboard:** Active campaigns (Insta, FB, Meta Ads).
- **Metrics Tracking:** Spend, Reach, CPL (Cost Per Lead).
- **Conversion Tracking:** Campaign -> Leads -> Interviews -> Joined.
- **Source Analysis:** Which organic/paid channels are performing best.
